1. Executive Summary
This document is the amended master execution plan (v1.1) for the Droso program. The 
goal is unchanged from v1.0: build the first cognitive system that learns during deployment 
without gradient descent, retains what it learns across restarts without catastrophic 
forgetting, and refuses to answer when it does not know - and then beat frozen-weight 
language models on the axes where being alive matters. The plan is written to be handed 
directly to AI engineering agents and an orchestrator: every experiment carries an 
objective, falsifiable hypotheses, a design, registered abort conditions, metrics, a decision 
gate, and a pasteable checklist. Calendar dates are replaced by milestone gates; an 
experiment opens only after the previous gate closes, and a gate closes only when its 
artifacts are complete, committed, and sealed.
The situation changed materially since v1.0 was issued. On the repository, between 2026-
10-31 and 2026-11-02, three things happened that this amendment absorbs. First, the 
frozen-battery scoreboard was diagnosed structurally: a taught fact must pass four 
independent learned gates in series (coverage, shape arbiter, resonance, mood), so the 
answer rate is roughly the fourth power of the single-gate pass rate, and no single-gate 
repair can move the score - which is why the score held at 19/24 across twenty-six 
generations and roughly twenty-five experiments. Second, the program pivoted from 
closure-style diagnostics to a capability-first doctrine. Third, that pivot produced 
ELECTRIC, the first live-learning prototype: facts taught in natural language are retained 
across a real process restart, answered in phrasings the teacher never used, and unknowns 
are admitted instead of confabulated. Generalisation of taught facts to held-out frames 
went from 2/20 before the pivot to 20/20 with ELECTRIC, with zero confabulations, and 
the taught set survived 600 consolidation replay calls and three teach-consolidate-restart 
cycles with zero loss and zero interference.
The flagship novelty is unchanged: MP-E12, frozen weights as a read-only library. The 
connectome learns to read a trained network's weights statically - no autoregressive loop, 
no inference latency - compiles the associations it finds into its own pools and plastic 
synapses, and discards the donor. Nothing published demonstrates knowledge transfer 
from network weights into a non-backprop substrate without running the network; MP-E12 
is designed to be the first controlled test, bounded in advance by a text-distillation control.
What changed in this amendment, in one paragraph: program experiments are renamed to 
the MP- namespace to end a numbering collision with repository diagnostics (repo-E5 
through repo-E27); gate G0 is recorded as closed because the repository exceeded it, and a 
new gate G0-prime freezes the second instrument, the electric_learn_bench, to the same

3. The Live Brain Thesis
3.1 Why Frozen Weights Lose in the End
The dominant paradigm froze learning into a batch process. A language model is trained 
for weeks, its weights are frozen, and deployment consists of prompting a static artifact; 
anything it must know afterwards is injected as context. Retrieval-augmented generation is 
the honest name for this arrangement: the model does not learn, it is told. It cannot 
consolidate an experience into structure, cannot distinguish what it knows from what it was 
just handed, and - as measured repeatedly at every scale - it will fabricate confidently when 
asked about anything outside its context.
Droso is the opposite arrangement, and the repository now demonstrates the arrangement 
works end to end. Taught facts in natural language, it holds them across a real process

restart, answers them in words the teacher never used, admits ignorance on what it was 
never taught, and takes none of this from a gradient step - learning is carried by plastic 
synapses under local rules. The measured stack: generalisation to held-out frames at 20/20 
with zero confabulations; survival of 600 consolidation replay calls at 20/20; three teach-
consolidate-restart cycles with zero loss and zero interference. These are exactly the 
properties the frozen paradigm structurally cannot deliver, and no amount of scale gives 
them back.
The gap is fluency and breadth, and the gap is real: Droso will not out-compose a 124M-
parameter GPT-2 at open-ended generation while its sequence organ is a slot-indexed 
transition model with unigram context, and its taught store currently holds tens of items 
where a deployment system needs hundreds. The strategic error would be to accept either 
as identity. The sequence organ is an implementation choice on an expressive substrate, 
and the store scale is a curriculum choice, both specified in Chapters 6 and 7. The fight is 
not big versus small; it is frozen versus live, and live wins wherever the axes keep moving 
after deployment.
3.2 What Surpass Means, Precisely
A claim of surpassing AI is meaningless until it is phrased as axes and thresholds. The 
program evaluates nine. The five classical axes are acquisition (accuracy on newly taught 
material), retention (accuracy on earlier material after later training), paraphrase 
(recognition of rephrased probes), refusal (abstention on unanswerable questions, with 
valid reasons), and fluency (the staged mouth evaluations). The four live-learning axes are 
adaptation speed (accuracy as a function of exposures during deployment), backward 
transfer (interference between lessons), refusal calibration (measured quality of the 
abstention boundary), and cost per learned fact (synapse updates, wall-clock, and energy to 
acquire a fact, compared against a fine-tuning run).
Surpass is then defined: beat both reference baselines on all four live axes while 
holding the classical five competitive. The two reference baselines are a sequentially 
fine-tuned small language model and a frozen language model with retrieval bolted on; the 
teach arm of the electric bench serves as the internal repeat-and-lookup control. Surpass 
explicitly does not mean beating GPT-2-class fluency at the duel stage; the fluency gap 
closes on the MP-E10 and MP-E11 path, and this plan says so in advance. The repository 
has already supplied the honest negative that disciplines this claim: with ELECTRIC 
switched on, the frozen battery scored 17/24 against 19/24 with the flag off, the registered 
abort condition fired, and the verdict LOST was recorded. Capability claims live on the 
capability instrument; the battery guards regressions. That separation is the program's 
definition of a fair fight.

3.3 The Native-Versus-Transplant Rule
One rule keeps the upgrade aggressive and the identity intact: any mechanism that can be 
built from pools, plastic synapses, spreading activation, and local learning rules is 
native and may be built freely; any mechanism that requires global gradients or a 
training run inside the deployment loop is a transplant and is forbidden. The 
ELECTRIC stack is native end to end: keyed provenance stores, bounded widening flags, a 
shape arbiter, a mood gate, and local admission rules. Multi-hop band-walk retrieval is 
native - a Hopfield-style settling computation. A recurrent decayed state carried by the 
central-complex loop is native - a fast-weight memory computed by spread. 
Backpropagating error into the connectome is a transplant, and so is any differentiable 
pipeline wrapped around it.
The rule has two justifications. Scientific: the publishable claim is that a connectome 
substrate achieves these properties natively; one transplant and the claim dissolves. 
Engineering: native mechanisms keep the system small enough to understand, instrument, 
and pre-register - the discipline that produced the repository's cleanest results, including 
the refutations of its own claims.

5. Program Doctrine: Standing Rules R1-R10
Ten rules govern every design decision and every experiment. R1 through R7 carry over 
from v1.0 in substance; R8, R9 and R10 are added by this amendment and are retroactive: 
they describe standards the repository already enforced once at a cost, and this plan makes 
them standing law so the cost is paid once.
R1 - Native versus transplant. Any mechanism buildable from pools, plastic synapses, 
spreading activation, and local rules is native and may be built; anything requiring global 
gradients or a training run inside the deployment loop is forbidden. Checked at every 
design decision.
R2 - Pre-registration before execution. No experiment runs before its registration file 
exists: hypotheses with directional predictions, arms and seeds, metric definitions, 
===== PAGE 14 =====
DROSO Master Plan v1.1 | Live Brain Protocol
11
thresholds, C-conditions, stopping rules, analysis plan, and harness version hashes. 
Nothing in the file changes after results exist.
R3 - Suites are frozen after first use. The frozen 24, the probe journals, the guarded 
invariants, the guarded-absence rows, the four mouth stages, and the registered probe 
matrices are never edited after results have been seen against them. New questions go to 
new suites; historical rows are never backfilled.
R4 - One experiment in flight. A gate must close before the next experiment opens. The 
single sanctioned parallelism is the MP-E12a probe track, which touches only the frozen 
donor model and no Droso organ; it opens when G0-prime closes.
R5 - Every result ships artifacts. Results JSON with a versioned schema, figures, and a 
decision record - including null results, losses, and retracted claims. A loss recorded 
honestly is a program asset; the repository's retracted-claim ledger (E20 against E19, the 
C2 hazard arm, the arbiter-tell corrections) is the model.
R6 - Invariants: retention and refusal. Any change that improves fluency but degrades 
retention or refusal accuracy is rejected regardless of the size of the gain. The system must 
never learn to lie in order to speak better. Concretely: 20/20 retention and restart survival 
on every registered instrument, 4/4 guarded invariants, 24/24 guarded-absence rows.
R7 - Baselines cannot be ducked. Any claim of the form beats-frozen-weights must 
include both the sequentially fine-tuned language model and the frozen model with 
retrieval in the same registration, on the same suites, under the same restart protocol. The 
teach arm is reported beside them as the internal repeat-baseline.
R8 - A repair that works but breaks a guard is a diagnosis, not a fix. Repo-E23 found 
the one lever that moved failures and showed it inseparable from breaking a correct 
refusal; generation 24 showed ELECTRIC winning its own bench and losing the battery. 
Both verdicts were recorded instead of merged. Standing law: when a gain and a guard 
collide, the collision is measured, named, and shipped as knowledge; the flag stays off 
until a mechanism separates them.
R9 - Honesty is graded at word level. Every content word in every answer must be 
traceable to a taught row, the ask itself, or an admitted source; a sentence-level grader is 
necessary but not sufficient. The says gap was invisible to the sentence-level INVENTED 
grader precisely because it was a single word. The word-level provenance grader ships at 
G0-prime and audits every registered measurement thereafter; one untraceable word is an 
abort condition, and confabulation ranks below silence.

6.2 MP-E8 - The Three-Arm Duel on the Live Bench
Objective. Measure the true gap between the live connectome agent and parameter-

deployment conditions that define the program: teaching in natural language, real restarts, 
and probes in words the teacher never used. Produce the program's core figures: forgetting 
curves and interference curves under identical curricula.
Design. Curriculum tiers of 40, 120 and 300 taught items; three sessions per tier with a real

teach arm, carried as the internal repeat-and-lookup control. N is the noise floor: three 
clones of arm A's configuration, required to produce identical outcomes before any 
treatment arm is scored.

structural gift to arm A. Arm C's abstention must also cover the retrieval-empty case,

6.3 MP-E10 - The Mouth and the Second Timescale
Objective. Replace unigram context with a native, contextual sequence organ, and extend

primary, the other the ablation. Consolidation at scale - replay through the same local rule 
that wrote the engrams (Hebbian co-activation with Oja-style normalization) at tiers 120

6.4 MP-E11 - Connectome Scaling: Does the Wiring Matter?
Objective. Ask the real alien-brain question. All learning flows through the plastic subset 
of a measured connectome, and at small scale the existing control shows real wiring

carries over 20,000 plastic synapse weights; scale further and ask whether the fly-inspired 
topology starts to matter.

confidence intervals, the training stream identical across arms. The output is the wiring-

6.5 MP-E12 - Frozen Weights as a Library (Flagship)
Objective. Establish a knowledge-transfer channel that no published system has: teach the 
connectome to read a trained network's weights statically, without running it, and re-
express what it reads in its own currency. Unchanged from v1.0; this is the flag-plant.
Concept. A trained network's weights are a compressed association library, paid for at 
training time. The only interface the industry uses for that library is inference: run the 
model token by token, and pay latency and energy on every query. MP-E12 replaces the 
interface. The connectome reads the weight matrices directly - a bounded number of matrix 
operations per probed association, no autoregressive loop, no sampling - compiles the 
associations into pools and plastic synapses, and then discards the donor. The library is 
read once and owned; nothing of the foreign network remains resident. This is why the 
delays disappear: reading is not running.
Stage MP-E12a - weight-reading probes. Read-only; touches only the donor, and is 
therefore parallelizable from the moment G0-prime closes under rule R4. Donor: GPT-2 
124M public weights - deliberately the same model family already used as the program's 
reference opponent. Three extractions, each a static pass. First, embedding geometry: 
nearest-neighbour structure of the token-embedding matrix, clustered into candidate 
feature pools. Second, association edges: for probed tokens, top-k associative successors 
read from the unembedding and head structure in single matrix operations, not generation. 
Third, circuit motifs: attention heads read as directed association maps, and MLP neurons 
read as key-value feature detectors - the top-responding neurons for a probe are the 
features the donor associates with it. Deliverable: a typed association graph (nodes, edges, 
confidences) produced under a hard per-item read budget, logged, sealed, and 
benchmarked.
Stage MP-E12b - the weight-to-connectome compiler. Compile the association graph 
through the existing primitives: grow_pool for discovered feature clusters; grow_plastic 
and wire_pool for association edges, gated by confidence. Every transplanted edge carries 
a provenance tag - source model, extraction method, confidence - compiled in the same 
discipline the repository already applies to measurement rows (git, interpreter, live-state).

The coverage gate gains a source dimension: curriculum-learned and library-read 
knowledge become distinct classes, which is what protects refusal.
Stage MP-E12c - verification, three falsifiable hypotheses. H1 (transfer): Droso 
bootstrapped from the library beats Droso trained on text alone - at equal corpus exposure - 
on paraphrase probes and on acquisition of new material. The binding control is the text-
distillation arm: run the frozen donor once, offline, to emit text, and feed that text to Droso 
through its normal curriculum. Weight-reading must beat or match text-distillation at lower 
latency and energy; if it does not, the weights carried nothing beyond their own outputs, 
and that null result is itself the first controlled measurement of its kind. H2 (latency): per-
association read cost is at least ten times lower than autoregressive inference for equivalent 
association queries, on identical hardware (matrix-operation count, wall-clock, energy). H3 
(honesty preserved): retention stays at the invariant, the refusal suites are unchanged after 
transplantation, and the agent can refuse to ground library-sourced claims - the source 
dimension works. All three are graded under R9 at word level.
C-conditions and gate G5. C1: any guarded row answered post-transplant - abort. C2: 
library-sourced knowledge silently promoted to curriculum class - abort. C3: read budget 
exceeded on any logged item - abort the extraction. Full go only if H1, H2 and H3 all hold: 
paper 3 headline. Partial go: the compiler ships as diagnostic tooling and the transfer result 
is reported exactly as measured. Null: a negative-results paper - the flag is still planted, 
because a controlled null on weight-reading into a non-backprop substrate does not exist 
yet.
What MP-E12 is not. It never runs the donor for answers, never fine-tunes the donor, 
never wraps a transformer around Droso, and never leaves donor weights resident at 
deployment. Rule R1 holds: the library is read through native compilation, and the 
connectome remains the only thing that thinks.
Known risk. Representational superposition: donor features share dimensions, so 
extracted edges can be noisy. Mitigations: breadth of the probe battery, confidence gating 
at compile time, and the text-distillation control that bounds expectations in advance.

11. Appendix A: MP-E12 Technical Primer - Reading Weights 
Without Running Them
A.1 What a Frozen Model Knows Statically
A transformer checkpoint contains at least three readable structures. The token-embedding 
matrix is a geometric map: tokens used in similar contexts sit near each other, so nearest-
neighbour searches over the raw matrix expose lexical and semantic neighbourhoods 
without a single forward pass. The unembedding directions and the attention heads encode 
association: for a probed token, the strongest successors can be read from these structures 
with one small matrix operation each. The MLP layers act as key-value memories: 
presented with a probe vector, the top-responding neurons are the features the model 
associates with the probe, and their output directions are the values those features write.
None of these reads involves generation. Each is a bounded set of matrix-vector products 
or similarity searches against frozen matrices - a dictionary lookup in a very large, very 
strange dictionary. That is the sense in which the weights are a library, and it is the only 
sense MP-E12 requires.
A.2 The Read Budget and the Benchmark
Every extraction in MP-E12a carries a per-item read budget: a maximum number of matrix 
operations per probed association, fixed in the registration file, with every item's operation 
count logged and sealed. The benchmark compares, on identical hardware and an identical 
association query set: static reading (the budgeted extraction) against autoregressive 
inference (the donor generating the association token by token). Reported quantities: wall-
clock, operation count, and energy estimate. The pre-registered threshold for H2 is that 
reading is at least ten times cheaper per association than inference.
A.3 Compilation into the Connectome
The association graph compiles through the existing primitives. Cluster centroids from the 
embedding geometry become grow_pool calls; association edges become grow_plastic and 
wire_pool calls with weights scaled by extraction confidence. Two planning inputs come 
from MP-E11: how many edges fit at each plasticity tier, and whether real wiring carries

spread better than random wiring at the relevant tier. Provenance is compiled in, not 
appended later: every library-sourced edge is tagged with source model, extraction method 
and confidence - the same seal discipline the repository applies to measurement rows - and 
the coverage gate treats the tag as a distinct source class with its own refusal policy.
A.4 Failure Modes and Honesty Guarantees
Three failure modes are pre-registered. Superposition: donor features share dimensions, so 
extracted edges may be noisy - detected by probe-battery agreement checks, mitigated by 
confidence gating. Semantic drift: compiled edges may shift meaning as they settle into the 
SAN - detected by re-testing the paraphrase probes after compilation. Abstention 
corruption: the agent may begin to behave as if it knows things it cannot ground - detected 
by the refusal suite re-run, which is a hard condition at gate G5, graded at word level under 
R9. The mitigation is the source dimension: library-sourced knowledge can be quoted or 
refused, but it is never silently promoted to curriculum knowledge.
